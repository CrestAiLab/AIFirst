

> **System Goal:** Redesign [https://ai-first-kappa.vercel.app/](https://ai-first-kappa.vercel.app/) into a professional AI Infrastructure & Community Hub.
> 
> **Reference Styles:**
> - **Structure:** XMPro (Industrial/Professional) + Public Sector Network (Community/Events).
> - **Vibe:** LunarTech (Futuristic/Clean) + SuperDataScience (Educational/Accessible).
> 
> **Core Value Proposition:** "We handle the Infrastructure; You handle the Innovation."
> 
> **Section-by-Section Engineering:**
> 
> 1. **Hero Section (The "Ease" Factor):**
>    - **Headline:** "AI Infrastructure Made Simple. Zero to Production in Minutes."
>    - **Subtext:** "From students to enterprise researchers—we provide the Dagster orchestration, Vector/Graph databases, and RAG support so you can focus on the work that matters."
>    - **Primary CTA:** "Book a Consultation" (Lead Gen focus).
>    - **Secondary CTA:** "Explore the Knowledge Hub."
> 
> 2. **Infrastructure Bento Grid (The "Tech Stack"):**
>    - Showcase modular icons for **Dagster, Vector DBs, Graph RAG, and AI Scanning Tools**.
>    - Use `ui-ux-pro-max-skill` **HoverCards** that reveal "One-click deployment" details when hovered.
> 
> 3. **Cyber-Security & Scan Hub (NEW):**
>    - A dedicated section with a "Scanning" visual animation.
>    - Feature: "AI-Powered Security Scan Tool" + "Knowledge Sharing Repository."
>    - Style: Clean, high-trust, dark-mode dashboard look.
> 
> 4. **Community & Learning Pathways:**
>    - Three distinct entry points (Use **ui-ux-pro-max-skill** Card components):
>      - **Students:** "Start your AI Journey (Zero Background Required)."
>      - **Tech Enthusiasts:** "Optimize & Build with our Infrastructure."
>      - **Businesses:** "Consulting & Enterprise Scaling."
> 
> 5. **Social Proof & Network Stats:**
>    - A horizontal "Trust Bar" showing: **Network Size | Projects Hosted | Setup Time Saved (e.g., "From 5 Days to 5 Minutes")**.
> 
> 6. **The "Forum/Discussion" UI:**
>    - A clean, card-based feed for "Latest Insights & Discussion."
>    - Integrate Sanity CMS to pull recent posts about #RAG, #Orchestration, and #CyberSecurity.
> 
> **UI/UX Requirements:**
> - **Palette:** Professional Navy/Slate base with "Security Green" or "Innovation Blue" accents.
> - **Components:** Use `ui-ux-pro-max-skill` for **Sticky Headers**, **Smooth Section Transitions**, and **Responsive Grids**.
> - **Experience:** Eliminate friction. Every "Technical" section should have an "Easy" explanation for general users.
> 
> **Output:** A Next.js 14+ layout using Tailwind CSS that prioritizes fast loading and professional lead generation.
